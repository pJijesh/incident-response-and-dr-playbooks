# Exercises

Plans only work if people have rehearsed them. This folder holds a ready-to-run tabletop exercise pack.

| Pack | Scenario | Duration | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ransomware tabletop](ransomware-tabletop/README.md) | Ransomware with data theft at a fictional freight forwarder, discovered late on a Friday | 2 hours (90 minute and 45 minute variants) | IT, operations, finance, customs or compliance, HR, communications, executive sponsor |

Also worth knowing: the NCSC offers a free online tool called Exercise in a Box, with ransomware, phishing and supply chain exercises, listed in [references](../docs/references.md#exercising). Use it alongside this pack, or instead of it.
