### Behaviours to watch for

- [ ] Someone stated "I would check the playbook" and found the right page quickly.
- [ ] Roles were named, and a deputy was identified for each.
- [ ] Time of awareness was recorded.
- [ ] Participants asked "how do we contact X if email is down?"
- [ ] Participants challenged each other, and no one dominated.
- [ ] Legal and DPO input was sought before notifying external parties.
- [ ] Workarounds for customs, warehouse and customer service were practical.
- [ ] The team discussed rest and handover for a long incident.
