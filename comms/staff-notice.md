# Staff notice template

**Use for:** the first message to all staff and any later updates. Send through the out-of-band channel if email or chat may be affected.

**Approval:** Incident Manager. Communications Lead sends.

---

**Subject: {{IT incident: short description}} (update {{number}}, {{time}})**

Hello everyone,

**What is happening**
{{One or two sentences in plain language, for example "We are dealing with a suspected cyber attack on our systems" or "Our freight management system is unavailable because of a problem at the supplier."}}

**What it means for you**
- {{Which systems or services are affected}}
- {{Which systems or services are working}}
- {{Workaround, for example "Use the paper pick lists at the warehouse" or "Call customers rather than emailing"}}

**What we need you to do**
- Do **not** use {{affected systems}} until we tell you they are safe.
- Do **not** switch off or restart your computer unless an IT team member asks you to.
- Do **not** open attachments or links from unexpected messages. Report anything suspicious to {{contact}}.
- Do **not** talk to the media or post on social media about the incident. Pass any enquiries to {{Communications Lead}}.
- If you see anything unusual, such as ransom messages, files that will not open or odd sign-in prompts, tell {{IT contact and number}} straight away. You will not be blamed for reporting.

**What we are doing**
{{One or two sentences: investigating, containing, working with the supplier or specialists.}}

**When you will hear from us next**
{{Time of next update, and the channel.}}

Thank you for your patience and help.

{{Name, role}}

---

## Variants

Subject: {{Incident name}} resolved. {{Systems}} are back in use.

{{What was restored, what is still limited, what staff must do (for example reset a password), where to ask questions, and a thank-you.}}

**Reminder message after a lost device**

Subject: Reminder: report lost or stolen devices straight away

If you lose a laptop, phone or security key, or think it has been stolen, call {{IT number}} immediately, day or night. Quick reports let us lock the device and protect data. You will not be in trouble for reporting.
