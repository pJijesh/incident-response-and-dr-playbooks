# Communication templates

Templates for the messages you will need under pressure. Pre-approve them with your executive sponsor, legal adviser and Data Protection Lead so that, in an incident, you are filling in blanks and not drafting from scratch.

## Writing principles

1. **Say what you know, what you do not know, and when you will next update.** Then keep that promise, even if the update is "no change".
2. **Be accurate before being fast, but do not be silent.** An early honest holding message is better than a late perfect one.
3. **No speculation** about cause, attacker identity or data loss until confirmed.
4. **Plain language.** No jargon, no blame, no false reassurance, no promises you cannot keep.
5. **One voice.** The Communications Lead issues external messages. Staff pass enquiries on.
6. **Do not use compromised channels.** If email or chat may be compromised, use the agreed out-of-band channel.
7. **Record what was sent**, when, to whom, and who approved it, in the [decision log](decision-log-template.md).
8. **Approval.** External messages need Executive Sponsor approval, and legal or Data Protection Lead review where personal data or liability is involved.

Fields to complete are written as `{{like this}}`.

> Templates are not legal advice. Take legal advice on content where liability, contracts or regulatory duties are involved.
