# Regulator notification template (ICO personal data breach)

**Use for:** preparing the content of a personal data breach report to the Information Commissioner's Office, so that the online form or phone call takes minutes, not hours.

**Important:** this template prepares your answers. The ICO's own form and guidance are the authority. Use the ICO's online breach report or its advice line, and the ICO's self-assessment tool if you are unsure whether to report. Links are in [references](../docs/references.md#data-protection-and-the-ico). This is not legal advice.

**Timing:** report without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware, if the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals. You may report in phases when you do not yet have all the facts. If you report after 72 hours, explain the delay.
