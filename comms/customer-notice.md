# Customer and partner notice template

**Use for:** informing customers, carriers and partners about service disruption or a data incident.

**Approval:** Executive Sponsor, with legal and Data Protection Lead review if personal data or contractual liability is involved.

**Check first:** contract notification clauses, service level terms, and whether the person you are writing to is the data controller (in which case they may have their own legal clock).

---

## A. Service disruption

**Subject: Service update: {{service}} ({{date and time}})**

Dear {{customer name}},

We are writing to let you know that {{service}} is currently {{unavailable or running slowly}} since {{time}}.

**What this means for you**
{{Impact in practical terms, for example "You may not be able to track shipments online. Your goods are not affected."}}

**What we are doing**
{{Short and truthful. For example "Our technical team is working with the supplier to restore the service."}}

**What you can do now**
{{Workaround, for example "Contact our customer service team on {{number}} for shipment status."}}

**Next update**
We will update you by {{time}}, even if there is no change.

We are sorry for the disruption. {{Contact name and number}}.

{{Name, role}}

## B. Data incident affecting personal data

Use this only after the Data Protection Lead has assessed the incident. Plain language matters most.

**Subject: Important information about your personal data**

Dear {{name}},

We are contacting you because we have found that {{brief, factual description of what happened and when}}.

**What information was involved**
{{Categories of data affecting this person, for example name, email address, delivery address. State clearly what was not involved, if known, such as financial details or passwords.}}

**What this could mean for you**
{{Likely consequences in plain language, for example "You may receive suspicious emails that use your details."}}

**What we have done**
{{Containment and remedial actions.}}

**What you can do**
- {{Specific advice, for example be alert to unexpected emails, calls or messages that mention your shipments, and do not click links in them.}}
- {{Change your password for {{service}} and for any other site where you use the same password.}}
- {{Where relevant, check bank statements.}}

**Who to contact**
{{Name or team, phone number, email address, opening hours.}}

We have {{reported or will report}} this to the Information Commissioner's Office, as required. {{Delete if not reported.}} We are very sorry that this has happened.

{{Name, role}}

## C. Supplier-targeted warning (business email compromise)

**Subject: Warning: fraudulent emails sent from {{company}} addresses**

We have identified that criminals sent emails that appeared to come from {{company}} between {{dates}}. These may ask you to change bank details or to pay an invoice to a different account. **Please do not act on them.** If you received such a message, contact {{name}} on {{known number}} to confirm. We will never change our bank details by email alone, and we will always confirm changes by telephone using a number you already hold.
