# Media holding statement template

**Use for:** responding to journalists or posting publicly when an incident is, or may become, public. Do not issue proactively unless the incident is already public or customers need to know.

**Approval:** Executive Sponsor, with legal review. Issued only by the Communications Lead. All staff are told to refer enquiries to them.

**Principles:** confirm what is certain, show you are acting, avoid speculation, avoid naming the attacker or blaming a supplier, do not discuss ransom or negotiations, and give a contact and next update time.

---

## Statement: cyber incident

> We are aware of an incident affecting {{some of our IT systems}} and have taken immediate action to protect our systems and data. {{We have informed the relevant authorities, including the Information Commissioner's Office and the National Cyber Security Centre. Delete if not yet done.}}
>
> Our priority is {{our customers, our people and restoring services safely}}. {{We are working with external specialists.}} Some services {{are currently disrupted / are running as normal}} and we are contacting those affected directly.
>
> We will provide further information when we are able to do so. {{Media enquiries: name, phone, email.}}

## Statement: data incident

> We have become aware that {{brief factual description}}. We are investigating with the help of {{specialists}}, and have {{contained the issue / taken steps to secure the data}}.
>
> {{We are contacting people who may be affected directly, and have notified the Information Commissioner's Office.}} If you think you may be affected, please contact {{contact route}}.
>
> {{Company}} takes the protection of personal data very seriously. We will update this statement at {{time or date}}. {{Media enquiries: name, phone, email.}}

## Statement: supplier or service outage

> {{Service}} is currently unavailable following a problem with a third-party provider. We are working with the provider to restore service as quickly as possible and are helping customers to keep their goods moving in the meantime. We will update at {{time}}. {{Contact}}.

## Do and do not

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Give one named contact | Let several people speak |
| Be consistent with what staff and customers have been told | Say "no customer data was affected" before it is known |
| Update at the time you promised | Speculate on cause, attacker or numbers |
| Keep a log of enquiries and replies | Discuss payment of any ransom |
| Check with legal before naming people or suppliers | Share names or details of affected individuals |

## Reactive Q and A (prepare with leadership)

| Likely question | Suggested approach |
|---|---|
| What happened? | Restate the approved facts. "We are investigating" is acceptable |
| Has data been stolen? | "We are working to establish whether any data was affected. We will tell those affected directly" (adjust to facts) |
| Have you paid a ransom? | "We do not comment on discussions with criminals." Take legal advice before saying more |
| Who is responsible? | "It would be wrong to speculate while investigations continue" |
| When will services be back? | Give only a timeframe you can stand behind. Otherwise commit to the next update time |
